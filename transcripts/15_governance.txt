Risk classification: High
  The system reads appointment records tied to identifiable patients (status, specialty, fees, follow-up need) and answers questions about clinical services such as emergency protocol, prescription refills and telemedicine eligibility. The brief's scheme lists medical data as High risk. A wrong or ungrounded answer could delay a patient seeking care, and a data leak would be a health-privacy breach. High risk is why the system masks PII before any processing or logging, refuses when retrieval does not support an answer, routes every answer through an independent review team, restricts record access to one agent, caps per-request spend, and writes an audit log.
  - data: Fixed-format PII (phone numbers) masked before agents, memory, cache and logs
  - model: Deterministic MOCK_LLM; extractive answers; groundedness gate; AutoGen review
  - application: Least autonomy: only the Lookup Agent holds check_appointment_status; no delegation
  - runtime: Per-request token/cost cap; JSONL audit log with trace IDs; response cache
